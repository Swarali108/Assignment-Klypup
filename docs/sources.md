## Signal sources

Where the app looks for new money events (`config/sources.py`, `config/signals.py`; status, last check, counts and
errors live on the **Sources** page, admin only). NSE and BSE are read on the owner's ruling although their terms
forbid automated collection (decision_log.md, row 15); every exchange request goes through the guardrails: at least
2 seconds between requests to a site, every response cached (`tools/fetch.py`), only new items fetched, an honest
User-Agent naming the app, ordinary session cookies from a home-page visit, no proxies, no IP rotation, no CAPTCHA
solving, and a 401, 403 or 429 stops that source for the run (shown as "blocked").

| Source | What it gives | Terms | On / off | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEBI · Red herring documents filed with ROC | IPO red herring prospectuses → IPO events | [Website policy](https://www.sebi.gov.in/website-policy.html): linking needs no permission; robots.txt allows the page | On | Low: titles, dates and links only |
| SEBI · Final offer documents filed with ROC | IPO prospectuses (supersede the RHP) | as above | On | Low |
| SEBI · Buybacks: tender offers | Buyback letters of offer and public announcements → buyback events | as above | On | Low |
| SEBI · Takeovers: letters of offer | Open offers → listed as "acquisition watch", not processed yet | as above | On | Low |
| SEBI · RSS | Anything SEBI publishes; sorted by title rules, then the cheap model | as above | On | Low |
| SEBI · Draft offer documents filed with SEBI | DRHPs → "IPO coming" watchlist items (early warning; nothing sold yet, never an event) | as above | On | Low |
| NSE · announcements, IPO lists, deals, insider disclosures | Allotment and listing notices (attached to the IPO event), OFS notices, buyback and acquisition notices, block and bulk deals, insider (PIT/SAST) sales → structured events | [Terms of use](https://www.nseindia.in/nse-terms-of-use): forbid "any systematic or automated data collection" | `EXCHANGE_SOURCES=on` (default locally) | High: against the site's terms; likely blocked from cloud servers |
| BSE · announcements, deals, insider disclosures | The same signals from BSE | [Disclaimer](https://www.bseindia.com/rgess/disclaimer.htm): forbids reproduction or redistribution | `EXCHANGE_SOURCES=on` (default locally) | High: against the site's terms |

Switch the exchanges off with `EXCHANGE_SOURCES=off`; discovery then reads SEBI only and the Sources page shows NSE
and BSE as off.
