# Assumptions

Where the brief left something open, I wrote down a working assumption and kept going. Each row says what the code does because of it, so a different answer from the client points at the exact thing to change.

| # | Ambiguity in the brief | Working assumption | Where it shows in the system |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Who **just came into money**" in an IPO | Only people **selling in the offer for sale** get cash. Fresh-issue money goes to the company. Everyone else on the cap table gets *paper* wealth: shares that become sellable when their lock-in ends | Call tiers: *Cash in hand* (sellers) vs *Just unlocked* vs *Holds shares* (`config/ranking.py`) |
| 2 | "Timing: money in hand now, or arriving later" | **Now** means sale proceeds paid, or due, within 30 days. **Later** means locked-in shares, with the unlock date taken from the lock-in rule printed in *this* filing, never from memory of SEBI's rules (these differ for SME and main-board issues, and change) | `CASH_NOW_DAYS = 30`; `agents/rule_sources.py` cites the lock-in sentence |
| 3 | "A ₹ range rather than one number" | A range wherever an input is uncertain: a price band (₹99–105), "up to N shares", or a holding valued at the offer price. When every input is a printed final figure, I show one number with its working. Adding ±10% around a certain figure would itself be an invented number | `amount_display`, e.g. "up to ₹1.70 crore, if all offered shares are sold" |
| 4 | "Confidence: how sure you are it's the right person" | It is about **identity**, not size: high = name and holding agree across more than one table; medium = verified in fewer tables; low = holder type or name match unsure. Kept apart from the amount | `agents/people.py:confidence()`; shown as "How sure" on the person card |
| 5 | "People an RM should call" | Individuals and family vehicles (HUFs, family trusts), because the RM calls the family. Funds and companies go to a separate organisations list: an RM cannot call a fund; the people behind it are later work | `CALLABLE_TYPES` in `config/ranking.py` |
| 6 | Promoter families split across relatives, trusts and holding companies | Group them into a **household** and flag the link, but never merge them: each member's amount stays separate and sourced | `family_groups()` in `agents/identity.py`; "Dharamshi household · 2 people" in the UI |
| 7 | Employees with ESOPs | Only people **named** in a filing appear. An option pool with a size but no names produces no rows. I never fill in names | KMP and ESOP sections are located but not yet extracted (a known gap, see `whats_broken.md`) |
| 8 | "Same person, different names" | Code rules first (same tokens after spelling rules; different first names with the same surname are different people; different fund numbers are different funds). A strong model is asked only about close pairs that are left. An unsure pair is flagged, not merged | `agents/identity.py`; strong model only at a fuzzy score of 85 or more |
| 9 | "Decide how far your system follows follow-on leads" | The pipeline searches only events it has already processed, one level deep, at most 10 matches per person, with no web search. The chat deep dive adds a $ budget, a visited set and caps per kind of branch | Stop rules in `tools/people_index.py`, printed in each `report.md` |
| 10 | "A buyback where founders, early investors or employees **were paid**" | A tender-offer buyback pays only holders who tender and are accepted. Promoters who state they will *not* tender get nothing, so they are context, not calls | Buyback non-tenderers sit in the `context` list, with a reason |
| 11 | "One of your choice that tests something the other two don't" | A **promoter sale through the stock exchange (OFS)**. There is no prospectus, only the company's 19-page notice to the exchanges: no seller tables in the usual places, no lock-ins, and settlement within days | `events/spacenet_ofs.json`, event type `ofs_stock_exchange` |
| 12 | "Public data only" and "no scraping where terms forbid it" | SEBI filing lists may be read automatically. NSE and BSE may not (their terms say so). A single exchange PDF can still be **added by hand** as an event document, as a person would download it | `config/sources.py` holds each source's terms sentence and its on/off state |
| 13 | "Cost: per event and per person" | Model cost only (not hosting). A brief is charged to its person; a name-pair check is split between the two people; a section's extraction is split across the people who kept a value from it; anything else is split across everyone in the event | `outputs/<event>/cost.json`, `per_person` |
| 14 | "Aim for under $2 per event" | Measured on a **fresh** run, with every model call counted as if nothing were cached. Repeat runs are reported separately | Fresh runs cost $0.002–$0.029 per event, as of 15 Nov 2026 |
| 15 | "The last 12 months" | Counted back from the brief's date (10 Nov 2026), on the event's listing or offer date | SEDEMAC listed 11 Mar 2026, the Spacenet OFS was in Apr 2026, Garware's letter of offer is from May 2026, and the others are from Oct–Nov 2026 |
| 16 | "A link for every fact" | The link opens the filing at the exact PDF page, with the quote highlighted. A computed figure (shares × price) links to each of its inputs. A fact without a verified quote is not shown, or is shown as "unknown" with the reason | Evidence chips on every ₹ figure |
| 17 | "No personal contact details" | Nothing beyond the name, role, holdings and transactions printed in filings. No phone numbers, emails or addresses are collected, even when a filing prints a registered office | The extraction schemas (`agents/extract_schemas.py`) have no field for contact details |
| 18 | "Run it live on an event you haven't seen" | The new event arrives as one or more public document links. The final price may not be printed yet (RHP only), so amounts stay a band-based range until a prospectus or allotment notice is added | "Add event from links" in the app, or `events/<id>.json` plus `run.py --step all` |
